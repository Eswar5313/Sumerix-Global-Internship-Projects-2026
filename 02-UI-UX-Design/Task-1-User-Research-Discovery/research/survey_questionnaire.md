# ShiftLoop — Survey questionnaire (Google Forms-ready)

Audience: hub team leads, associates, dock/linehaul supervisors and hub managers. Target: **≥ 5 responses (aim for 15)** across all three shifts.
Consent line for the form header: *Answers are anonymous and used only for a UX internship project. You can skip any question.*

| # | Question | Type | Options / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | What is your role at the hub? | Single choice | Team Lead · Associate · Hub Manager · Linehaul / Dock supervisor · Other |
| Q2 | Which shift do you usually work? | Single choice | Morning · Evening · Night · Rotating |

## Interview guide (30 min, 1:1, at the hub)

1. Warm-up (2 min): role, hub, shift pattern, years in logistics.
2. Walk me through the last handover you GAVE — start to finish. (observe tools, time, people)
3. Walk me through the last handover you RECEIVED. What did you check first?
4. Tell me about a time something important was not passed on. What happened next? Who fixed it?
5. Where does shift information live today? (show me the WhatsApp group / register if allowed)
6. What do you do when the network is down at the dock?
7. Who is accountable when an exception is missed? How is that decided?
8. If you had a magic button for handover, what would it do?
9. Wrap-up: anything we didn't ask? Can we observe a live handover?

## Observation checklist (live handover)

- Start/end time of the handover conversation
- Tools touched (phone, register, scanner, whiteboard)
- Items mentioned verbally but not written anywhere
- Interruptions (vehicle arrival, calls)
- Who signs / confirms, if anyone
