# Problem statement

Night- and evening-shift team leads at gateway hubs need a fast, structured way to hand over pending loads, exceptions, safety and equipment status to the incoming lead, because today that information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, paper registers and verbal briefings — so items are missed, nobody can prove who acknowledged a safety risk, and the next shift starts by firefighting instead of planning.

## How might we

- How might we let a tired team lead capture a complete handover in under 5 minutes, even without network?
- How might we make every critical item have an owner and a timestamped acknowledgement?
- How might we give hub managers an audit trail without asking leads to type twice?

## Target users

- Primary: night/evening-shift team leads at gateway hubs
- Secondary: incoming leads, hub managers, associates
