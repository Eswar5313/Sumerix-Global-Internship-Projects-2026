# Empathy map — Divya at handover

## Thinks
- “If I miss this, the morning shift will blame my team.”
- “Typing a long message now will delay dispatch.”
- “Did anyone even read my safety note?”

## Feels
- Pressured in the last 30 minutes of the shift
- Anxious about un-owned exceptions
- Proud when the next shift starts clean

## Says
- “Check the group, I posted it.”
- “Bag 4471 is pending — tell Arun.”
- “Forklift on D7 is down again.”

## Does
- Sends 10–20 WhatsApp messages and voice notes
- Writes a short note in the paper register
- Walks the docks for a last check, phone in hand

## Pain points
- Information scattered across chats and paper
- No acknowledgement or owner
- Network drops inside the dock
- Same data typed twice

## Gains
- Handover done in < 5 minutes
- Proof that every critical item was acknowledged
- Fewer calls after going home
- Recognition for clean shifts
