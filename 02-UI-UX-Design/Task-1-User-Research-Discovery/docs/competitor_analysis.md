# Competitor analysis

Heuristic review of public product pages (Nov 2026); validate hands-on.

| Criterion | Connecteam | Deputy | Microsoft Teams — Shifts | Status quo: WhatsApp group + paper register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary job | All-in-one frontline app: scheduling, time clock, checklists/forms, chat | Scheduling, timesheets and labour cost | Rosters and shift swaps inside Teams | Free-form chat + handwritten log |
| Navigation | 4/5 — Tab bar + module hub; many modules to discover | 4/5 — Clear schedule-first navigation | 3/5 — Lives inside Teams; several taps to reach Shifts | 2/5 — Chronological chat only; no structure |
| Layout & visual hierarchy | 4/5 — Card-based, configurable | 4/5 — Clean calendar/timeline views | 3/5 — Dense Office styling | 1/5 — Wall of messages; important items scroll away |
| Search | 3/5 — Search inside modules | 3/5 — People/shift search | 4/5 — Teams search across chats/files | 2/5 — Keyword search in chat; register not searchable |
| 'Checkout' = handover sign-off | 3/5 — Possible via custom checklist/form; not hub-specific | 2/5 — No dedicated handover template found on public pages | 2/5 — Notes per shift; no structured acknowledgement | 1/5 — No sign-off, no owner, no timestamp |
| Offline use at the dock | 3/5 — Varies by module | 3/5 — Some offline features | 2/5 — Needs connectivity for most actions | 4/5 — Paper always works; chat queues |
| Accessibility & language | 4/5 — Multiple languages; readable type | 3/5 — Good contrast; limited Indian languages | 4/5 — Office accessibility tooling | 3/5 — Familiar UI; voice notes help low-literacy users |
| Overall UX for a hub handover | 3/5 — Powerful but generic — needs set-up per site | 2/5 — Strong for rostering, weak for operational context | 2/5 — Fits office teams better than floor teams | 2/5 — Fast to start, fails on accountability |

## Strengths & weaknesses

| Product | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Connecteam | Checklists, forms, chat and scheduling in one app; frontline focus | Generic forms → each hub must design its own handover; can feel heavy for associates |
| Deputy | Mature scheduling, compliance and timesheet flows | Handover context (pending loads, exceptions, dock status) is outside its core model |
| MS Teams — Shifts | Already in many enterprises; strong identity & search | Office-centric UI; floor staff rarely live in Teams; weak offline story |
| WhatsApp + register | Zero training, works on any phone, voice notes | No structure, no ownership, no audit trail; safety items get buried |
